**Date:** 3 November 2026. **Branch:** `dark-story-rewrite`.

## Narrative improvements

- **Mercury Lighthouse:** Mia's uneasy tactical cooperation no longer requires instant trust; she acknowledges delaying the rescue rather than pretending a duel solved their distrust. Her pursuit remains motivated by saving the hostages.
- **Lamakan and Tolbi:** Mia takes responsibility for her anger after the Manticore without having the same confession repeated across every encounter. Isaac's defense respects her own ability to answer.
- **Venus Lighthouse:** Saturos and Menardi's final exchange hints that their people are depending on them, and notes that the other two beacons remain dark. Their tragedy becomes more visible without inventing a Prox exposition dump or changing the outcome.
- **Venus aftermath:** Mia's inability to help the fallen, Ivan's insistence on evidence, and Garet's fear are differentiated. Both response branches stay careful about the uncertainty of Felix and Sheba's survival.
- **Post-Venus Lalivero:** Ash Jenna is explicitly ruthless and willing to let a town burn for revenge. Isaac refuses to let her determine whose life matters, but he no longer repeatedly promises an impossible rescue of everyone. The Wise One's alleged guilt remains solely Ash Jenna's accusation. Her appearance remains optional, and the original Jenna/Kraden ending remains untouched.
